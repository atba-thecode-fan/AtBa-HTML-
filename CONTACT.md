# Contact

## Getting in Touch

We'd love to hear from you! Whether you have questions, suggestions, or just want to say hello, here are the ways you can reach us.

## Communication Channels

### GitHub Issues

For bug reports, feature requests, and general discussions:

- **Report a Bug:** Open an issue with the `bug` label
- **Request a Feature:** Open an issue with the `enhancement` label
- **Ask a Question:** Open an issue with the `question` label

Visit our [Issues page](https://github.com/atba-thecode-fan/AtBa-HTML-/issues) to get started.

### GitHub Discussions

For general discussions and community conversations:

- Visit our [Discussions page](https://github.com/atba-thecode-fan/AtBa-HTML-/discussions)

### Email

For private matters or direct contact with maintainers:

- **Email:** atbatvarsivi@gmail.com

### Social Media

Follow us for updates and announcements:

- Stay tuned for social media links and community platforms

## Security Issues

**Do not** open public issues for security vulnerabilities. Please refer to our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) for responsible disclosure guidelines.

## Response Time

We aim to respond to inquiries within a reasonable timeframe, typically:

- **Issues and Discussions:** 24-48 hours
- **Security Reports:** As soon as possible (urgent priority)
- **Email:** Within a few business days

## Before You Contact Us

Please check the following before reaching out:

- Review the [README.md](README.md) for project overview
- Check existing [Issues](https://github.com/atba-thecode-fan/AtBa-HTML-/issues) to see if your question has been answered
- Review our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and ensure your communication follows our community standards
- For security concerns, follow our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md)

## Contributing

Interested in contributing? Check out our contribution guidelines and start making an impact!
