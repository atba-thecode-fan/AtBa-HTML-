# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our project and repository.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information

When you interact with this repository, we may automatically collect certain information, including but not limited to:

- GitHub username and profile information
- Commit history and contribution records
- Issue and pull request activity
- Access logs and IP addresses
- Browser type and device information

### Information You Provide

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as:

- Comments and discussions in issues and pull requests
- Communications with maintainers
- Any personal information included in commit messages or code

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- Managing and improving the project
- Communicating with contributors and users
- Tracking contributions and project activity
- Enforcing our Code of Conduct and terms of service
- Protecting against malicious, deceptive, or illegal activity
- Responding to legal requests and protecting our rights

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except:

- When required by law or legal process
- To protect the security and integrity of the project
- With your explicit consent

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

## GitHub's Privacy Practices

As this project is hosted on GitHub, your use is also governed by [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement). Please review GitHub's privacy policies for additional information.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

- The right to access your data
- The right to correct or delete your data
- The right to opt-out of certain data collection
- The right to data portability

For requests regarding your data, please contact the project maintainers.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the bottom of this policy.

## Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact the project maintainers.
